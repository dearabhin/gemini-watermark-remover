Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective for removewatermark.dev

Short version: We collect zero personal data. Your images never leave your device. We don't use sign-ups, accounts, or tracking cookies.

1. Who We Are

RemoveWatermark.dev is a free, open-source web tool built and maintained by Abhin Krishna, a student developer from Kerala, India. The tool removes the visible ✨ sparkle watermark from images generated by Google Gemini AI using a browser-based Reverse Alpha Blending algorithm.

GitHub: github.com/dearabhin/gemini-watermark-remover

2. Image Processing & Your Data

All image processing on this website is performed entirely within your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and JavaScript. This means:

  • Your images are never uploaded to any server.
  • Your images are never stored anywhere outside your device.
  • Your images are never shared with any third party.
  • No image metadata (filename, EXIF data, dimensions) is transmitted anywhere.

Once you close or refresh the page, any processed data is immediately cleared from memory.
